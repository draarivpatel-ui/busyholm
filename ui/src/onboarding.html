Welcome to Busyholm

A small papercut island lives along the bottom of your screen. Each coding agent you run becomes a resident that works at its own workshop. Once its hooks are connected, a resident rings the bell and raises a flag when your agent needs you. Busyholm ships for macOS at launch, with Windows and Linux later.

What Busyholm sees

Busyholm reads your agents' activity logs and transcripts to count events and never keeps, shows or sends their text, prompts, replies, code or file paths. No outbound network connections; loopback only.

Setup takes about a minute. You can change everything later in Settings.

Connect your agents

Hooks let a resident ring the bell and raise a flag when your agent needs you. Only event types, ids, timings and counts ever leave the agent process; content is discarded inside the shim, and Busyholm makes no outbound network connections; loopback only.

Choose what to connect

You will see the exact changes before anything is written. Busyholm keeps only what restoring needs (the exact entries it adds and any status line command it replaces) in install-manifest.json and backups/install/ inside its app data folder, readable only by your user account (0600). Disconnect removes them.

Here is exactly what changes

Nothing is written until you choose Connect. Busyholm marks every entry it adds and saves only what restoring needs in install-manifest.json and backups/install/ (owner-only, mode 0600), so Disconnect in Settings removes those entries and deletes those restore records.

One more step for Codex

Codex asks you to review new hooks before it runs them. This is Codex keeping you in charge of what runs on your computer.

  1. Open Codex in a terminal.
  2. Type /hooks and press Return.
  3. Look over the Busyholm entries and approve them.

Until you approve them, Codex residents still show their work on the island, but cannot ring the bell.

Start from your past activity?

Busyholm can read your agents' recent activity logs and transcripts to count sessions and finished turns and give the island a head start. It never keeps, shows or sends their text, prompts, replies or code.

Where the island lives

The island sits in a thin strip along the bottom of your screen, just above the Dock or taskbar. It never takes keyboard focus; the Busyholm window and the tray menu are there for keyboard use.
